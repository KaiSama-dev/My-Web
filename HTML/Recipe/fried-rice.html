About This Recipe

This is a simple fried rice recipe that is easy to make at home.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time 10 minutes
Cooking Time 15 minutes
Servings 2 people

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 egg
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a pan.
  2. Add the onion and cook for a few minutes.
  3. Add the egg and scramble it.
  4. Add the cooked rice.
  5. Add soy sauce, salt, and pepper.
  6. Mix everything well.
  7. Cook for 5 minutes and serve.
