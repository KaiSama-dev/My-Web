Homemade Burger Recipe

Home | Fried Rice | Pasta | Burger

About This Recipe

Learn how to make a simple homemade burger.

Ingredients

  • 2 burger buns
  • 2 beef patties
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Cheese
  • Mayonnaise

Instructions

  1. Cook the burger patties in a pan.
  2. Toast the burger buns.
  3. Add lettuce and tomato.
  4. Add the cooked patty.
  5. Add cheese and mayonnaise.
  6. Close the burger and serve.
