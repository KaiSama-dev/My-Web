About This Recipe

A simple and tasty pasta recipe for beginners.

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • Tomato sauce
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Cheese
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Boil water in a large pot.
  2. Add the pasta and cook until soft.
  3. Cook the onion and garlic in a pan.
  4. Add tomato sauce.
  5. Add the cooked pasta.
  6. Mix everything together.
  7. Add cheese and serve.
